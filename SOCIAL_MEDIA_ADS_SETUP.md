# Social Media Advertising Guide for Liprobakin Basketball League

## Overview
This guide will help you create effective social media advertising campaigns to promote your basketball league website and attract more fans, players, and visitors.

## 1. Facebook & Instagram Ads (Meta Business)

### Setup Steps:
1. **Create Meta Business Manager Account**: https://business.facebook.com
2. **Add Your Website**: business.facebook.com > Settings > Data Sources
3. **Create Facebook Pixel**: 
   - Go to Events Manager
   - Click "Connect Data Sources" > "Web" > "Facebook Pixel"
   - Replace `YOUR_PIXEL_ID` in layout.tsx with your actual Pixel ID
4. **Verify Domain**: Add domain to Business Manager for better ad delivery

### Campaign Types for Basketball League:
- **Brand Awareness**: Introduce your league to new audiences
- **Traffic**: Drive visitors to your website
- **Engagement**: Increase likes, shares, comments on your content
- **Lead Generation**: Collect emails for newsletter signups
- **Event Promotion**: Promote upcoming games and events

### Target Audiences:
- **Demographics**: 
  - Age: 16-45 years old
  - Location: Congo (DRC), Central Africa + Basketball fans globally
  - Interests: Basketball, Sports, NBA, Local sports teams
- **Custom Audiences**: 
  - Website visitors (requires Pixel setup)
  - Similar to existing fans
- **Lookalike Audiences**: Based on your best website visitors

### Ad Creative Ideas:
1. **Game Highlights Videos** (15-30 seconds)
2. **Player Spotlight Images** with stats
3. **Live Game Announcements** with venue and time
4. **Team Introduction Carousels**
5. **Behind-the-scenes Content**

### Budget Recommendations:
- Start with $5-10/day per campaign
- Increase based on performance
- Best performing: Weekend game promotions

## 2. Google Ads Setup

### Campaign Types:
1. **Search Campaigns**: Target people searching for basketball in Congo
2. **Display Campaigns**: Show banner ads on sports websites
3. **YouTube Ads**: Video ads before basketball content

### Keywords to Target:
```
basketball congo
basketball drc
liprobakin
basketball league africa
sports congo
basketball games live
congo basketball teams
```

### Setup:
1. Create Google Ads account: ads.google.com
2. Link with Google Analytics (already set up in your site)
3. Import conversions from your website

## 3. TikTok Ads

### Why TikTok for Basketball:
- High engagement for sports content
- Younger demographic (16-34)
- Video-first platform perfect for highlights

### Campaign Setup:
1. Create TikTok for Business account
2. Install TikTok Pixel (similar to Facebook)
3. Create short-form video content

### Content Ideas:
- Player trick shots
- Game winning moments
- Team warm-ups and training
- Fan reactions

## 4. Twitter (X) Ads

### Best for:
- Real-time game updates
- Live score promotions
- Basketball conversations

### Campaign Types:
- Promoted Tweets during games
- Follower campaigns
- Website traffic campaigns

## 5. YouTube Advertising

### Video Ad Types:
1. **In-stream ads**: Play before basketball videos
2. **Discovery ads**: Show in search results
3. **Shorts ads**: Vertical video format

### Content Strategy:
- Game highlights (30-60 seconds)
- Player interviews
- Season preview/recap

## Budget Allocation Suggestions

**Monthly Budget: $300-500**
- Facebook/Instagram: 40% ($120-200)
- Google Ads: 30% ($90-150)
- TikTok: 20% ($60-100)
- Twitter: 10% ($30-50)

## Key Metrics to Track

### Facebook/Instagram:
- Cost Per Click (CPC): Aim for $0.20-0.50
- Click-Through Rate (CTR): Target 2%+
- Cost Per Website Visit: $0.50-1.00

### Google Ads:
- Search campaigns CTR: 3%+
- Display campaigns CTR: 0.5%+
- Cost Per Click: $0.30-0.80
